
Quick Cheat Sheet for Common Prefixes

Using a short prefix in your commit messages keeps your paper's history easy to scan later:
• docs: Writing or editing text in sections (Intro.tex, Methods.tex, abst.tex)
• fix: Fixing typos, broken citations, equations, or formatting bugs
• feat: Adding new figures, tables, or sections
• refactor: Reorganizing macros, packages, or file structure (cmnds.tex, meta.tex)
If you don't already have a .gitignore file set up to keep LaTeX build clutter (.aux, .log, .bbl, .pdf) out of your GitHub repository, let me know and I can give you a ready-to-use one!
